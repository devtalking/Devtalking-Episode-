About DevTalking Episode

DevTalking Episode is a weekly 30-min podcast where builders and founders showcase what they've built to the web3 community.

Get insights from the forefront of bleeding-edge tech from founders and developers creating the next wave of disruptive innovations in web3 and AI. Broadcast to one of the most engaged developer communities in web3, DevTalking Episode provides a platform for developers to learn about new technologies and for founders to get feedback from the community.
